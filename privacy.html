Privacy Policy

Checkers · Draughts · Damas · Last updated: 2026-10-24

This policy explains what the mobile game Checkers · Draughts · Damas (“the App”, “we”) collects, why, and how you can delete it.

Summary

  • The App is free. Playing against the computer and 2 players on one device work offline.
  • We never ask for a password, contacts, photos or precise location. An email is asked only, and optionally, when you send feedback.
  • On first launch the App creates an anonymous guest account on our server for the leaderboard and reminders.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob.
  • You can delete your data at any time: Settings → Delete my data.

Information we collect

  • Guest account (our server): a random ID generated by the App (not a hardware or advertising ID), a user number, a sign-in token and a display name. The display name is generated automatically (for example “Player1085”) and you can change it.
  • Leaderboard: the number of games you win against the computer or online, all-time and per week. Your display name and wins are shown publicly on the leaderboard, so do not use your real name.
  • Notifications: a push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging, your platform (Android or iOS) and the App language, used to send occasional reminders. Daily and “come back” reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Online play and rooms with friends: your moves, the room code and your display name are sent to your opponent through our game server, or directly over your Wi-Fi / hotspot when you both play on the same network. They are not kept after the match ends. Scanning a room QR code uses your camera on your device only; images are not stored or sent.
  • Feedback (optional): when you send feedback in Settings, we receive your message, category, optional email, guest user number, App version, device model, system version and language. It is forwarded to our support chat (Telegram) so we can reply and fix problems.
  • On your device: your settings (language, difficulty, sound, variant, notifications).
  • Technical logs: our servers may record IP addresses and request times in short-lived logs for security and troubleshooting.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used (for example games started and finished, variant, difficulty, screens opened, App version, device type and approximate country). This data is collected with an app-instance identifier and the device’s advertising ID where available, is not used to identify you personally, and helps us improve the game and measure our advertising. In the EEA and the UK, advertising-related signals follow your choices in the privacy form shown by the App. Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Google AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers, the advertising ID and app usage to show and measure ads. In the EEA and the UK the App asks for your consent first; you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy options. Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Service providers

  • Google AdMob: ads and consent.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: usage statistics.
  • Telegram: delivering feedback messages to our support team.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your guest account and leaderboard results while you use the App. Settings → Delete my data permanently removes your account, leaderboard results and push token from our server. Uninstalling the App removes the data stored on your device.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service providers above, to run the App.

Children

The App is for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete my data.
  • Turn off reminders: Settings → Notifications, or in your device settings.
  • Ads: Settings → Ad privacy options (EEA/UK), or reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.
